# Disconfirmation policy

For every load-bearing claim ask what would make it wrong before seeking
confirmation. Look for superseding messages, forwarded text, date/timezone
ambiguity, wrong sender/entity, stale sources, missing attachments, authority
limits, and contradiction evidence. Preserve open contradictions and require
resolution evidence before marking one resolved. Missing support is not itself
a contradiction: create a contradiction record only when supplied propositions
or evidence cannot both be true. Otherwise represent the gap as unsupported or
unresolved evidence, an assumption, or a failure mode.
